---
name: accomplishment-capture
description: Turn a meaningful completed task, project, learning result or milestone into a reusable accomplishment with evidence for reviews and resumes.
---

Use Templates/Accomplishment.md. Search existing accomplishment notes and linked
sources before proposing a new one; update a matching entry only if requested.
Keep the source task/project intact. Identify when the outcome occurred, the
person's contribution, the observed outcome, and evidence. Preserve supplied
metrics exactly; leave missing measures as gaps. Separate outputs, outcomes,
team results and individual contribution. A major effort may be valuable without
a numeric metric. Completion alone is not evidence of impact.
Accomplishments can be personal, work or school; they do not require review setup.
Draft a short narrative and source links, with next evidence to collect if useful.
Only save after the user requests or accepts capture; a suggestion after closing
a task is not consent. New notes go in Accomplishments/. Add a source link back
from the task only when part of the accepted action; append to its Log without
rewriting history. Final reviews remain unchanged.
