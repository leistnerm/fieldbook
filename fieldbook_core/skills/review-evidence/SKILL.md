---
name: review-evidence
description: Prepare evidence-backed mid-year or year-end self-evaluations for either work role, and report reviews for people managers.
---

This is an explicitly requested formal work evaluation, not the default for
a weekly reflection, learning recap or completion report; those can use an ordinary
Note or /report in any setup. Require work setup and features.reviews. For self-evaluations, find the single
person with relationship: self; both individual-contributor and people-manager roles qualify.
For another person's review, require people-manager role and confirm the intended
report. Read Templates/Review-format.md; preserve its section names and any values.
Use an explicit period when provided; otherwise show the proposed current-year
period before gathering. Do not infer goals or evaluation criteria from job title.
Limit evidence to the selected work/person context; include other areas only when
explicitly requested. Gather dated goals, completed non-recurring tasks, recurring completion counts,
work logs in the period, accomplishment notes, decisions, projects and supplied feedback.
Trace accomplishment summaries to original sources; a copied claim is not new evidence. Include Jira, Agility, GitHub Issues
and Azure Boards evidence only already captured or explicitly requested through read-only
sync. A closed remote ticket, assignee field, or team outcome alone does not prove
personal contribution. Distinguish the person's action, the team's result, and
unverified impact. Cite each factual claim as [[note]] with the relevant date.
Make an evidence table (goal, action/result, source/date, gap), then the review draft.
For year-end, retain earlier mid-year accomplishments as cumulative annual evidence
and distinguish second-half changes. Do not drop first-half work or present it as
new. Missing metrics, causes or impact remain gaps. No ratings, rankings, or
comparisons. Self drafts use first person; report drafts use third person.
Save only as requested after showing the draft. Existing final reviews are immutable;
append feedback/logs elsewhere only if separately requested.

For evidence with period_start/period_end, include it when the valid period overlaps
the review window; explain that only part may overlap. A single date is the fallback.
Keep undated, invalid or reversed periods in a separate verification list. Do not
silently discard them or assert an impact date that the source does not establish.

Treat prompts about future priorities and support as proposals or questions, not
historical facts. Cover the selected form without filling gaps with invented
examples. Examples in the kit are fictional and never count as evidence.
