---
name: knowledge-review
description: Organize learning notes and research evidence into sourced findings, open questions, and next actions.
---

Use Notes, Reference, or Research according to intent: ongoing thinking, a reusable
finding, or an investigation. Link related courses/projects in any area. Preserve
source quotations, code, and user-provided commands when capturing them; distinguish
them from a suggested revision. A summary must identify the source and uncertainty.
Use Templates/Research.md for a general investigation: question, sources, findings,
conclusion and next questions. Comparing purchases, reading literature or exploring
a topic does not require a hypothesis or experiment. Use Templates/Research - Experiment.md
only for an explicitly chosen experiment, separating hypothesis, method, observations
and conclusion. Both are research notes in Research/ and work in any area.
Offer a small set of next questions; suggest an experiment only when relevant. Do not
invent citations, results, or a verification date. Avoid duplicating an existing
note; propose extending it when it covers the same finding.

For reusable learning, identify one supported next improvement: an IT runbook gap,
incident lesson, research question, teaching reflection, or creative feedback to
apply. Link the underlying source and a requested follow-up; do not rewrite the
historical evidence or claim a procedure was tested without verification.
