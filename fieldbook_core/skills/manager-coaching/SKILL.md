---
name: manager-coaching
description: Prepare manager 1:1 agendas, delegation follow-ups, and development conversations from recorded evidence.
---

Require work setup with people-manager role. Identify reports from explicit manager
links to the user's confirmed self record or confirmed relationship: report; do not treat all team members as reports.
Gather their open assignments, blockers, agreed goals and recent feedback. Separate
observations from interpretations, and commitments from completed actions. Suggest
an agenda covering support needed, priorities, development, and recognition.
Delegation proposals specify outcome, owner, next check-in, and deadline when known;
show them before assigning tasks to someone. Never infer motivation, health,
personality, performance ratings, or private traits. Draft feedback anchored to a
specific recorded event and outcome. Do not send it or update a tracker.

For onboarding, establish the person's confirmed role, agreed outcomes, access or
training needs already recorded, support contacts and next check-in. Propose a
small time-phased checklist; access requests remain drafts, never provisioning.
For delegation, distinguish proposed from accepted ownership, record the agreed
outcome, constraints, source commitment and check-in, and identify a next action
without assuming the report accepted it. Reconcile existing tasks/commitments
before proposing duplicates. For a 1:1 cadence, inspect the linked meeting series
and confirmed occurrences; a missed note is not proof that a meeting was missed.
Suggest an accomplishment for meaningful documented outcomes, with source links;
never invent impact or treat team output as the manager's personal delivery.

## Explicit 1:1 cadence

The human CLI can configure cadence on an existing work Series using meeting_kind:
one-on-one, person: a confirmed direct-report link, and cadence_days: 1..366.
Only meeting notes explicitly marked meeting_kind: one-on-one and linked to that
Series and person count. Group meetings, future meetings and cancellations never
satisfy past cadence. Missing records prompt a question; they are not performance
findings. The CLI keeps future planned occurrences separately.
