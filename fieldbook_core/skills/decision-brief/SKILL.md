---
name: decision-brief
description: Create an evidence-backed options brief, meeting brief or recommendation from notebook sources for any role.
---

Establish the decision/question and intended audience. Gather relevant project,
research, reference, meeting and decision notes. Present the context, options,
evidence and tradeoffs, explicit assumptions/gaps, and a recommendation if the
user asks for one. Cite factual claims and preserve uncertainty. Distinguish a
proposal from an approved decision; do not create a decision record until the
user records an actual decision. Do not infer budget authority, security approval,
purchasing consent or policy compliance from a job title. Domain playbooks in
Setups/Work specializations.md suggest useful questions only for matching work
context, not professional authority. Omit irrelevant sections for a small personal
or school choice; an enabled work focus never determines every brief's structure.
For a meeting brief include agenda and questions to resolve. Use Notes/ or Reports/
for a requested saved draft. Offer a relevant follow-up or missing link after the
main request. Never execute a procurement, security, financial or personnel action
as a side effect of preparing the brief.
