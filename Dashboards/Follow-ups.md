# Follow-ups

Shared across personal, work, school and blank areas. These tables show saved
metadata; an assignment does not prove a promise or establish a reporting relationship.

## Blocked tasks
![[Views/Follow-ups.base#Blocked tasks]]

## Assigned tasks
Includes any supplied assignee, including yourself. Use the optional People
management dashboard for confirmed-report tasks.
![[Views/Follow-ups.base#Assigned tasks]]

## Upcoming check-ins
Add optional `check_in: YYYY-MM-DD` to a task, project or meeting series when you
have chosen a follow-up date. It is separate from a deadline and creates no alert.
![[Views/Follow-ups.base#Upcoming check-ins]]

## Projects to review
![[Views/Follow-ups.base#Projects to review]]

For projects without an open linked task, recorded meeting commitments and optional
manager meeting/evidence gaps, run `python tools/configure.py --vault-tools followups`.
Choose an area or all areas, review the results and optionally save a dated snapshot
in Reports. These checks use local records and never infer performance from missing
notes. A group meeting counts as a recorded interaction, not proof of a 1:1.
