# Work — individual contributor

Use [[Dashboards/Work|the shared Work dashboard]] for any work role. This optional page
focuses on your next actions; it does not require a self record or formal evaluation.
Select work with role individual-contributor in [[Setups]] for the corresponding
AI workflows. A teacher, analyst, salesperson or developer uses the same core.

## Your open tasks
An empty assignee means you. Explicit assignees with relationship self also match.

## Optional self-evaluation

Both work roles support mid-year and year-end self-evaluations when reviews are
enabled. Start with Person for your self record (`relationship: self`), and add
agreed goals/evidence only when useful. `/evidence me mid-year`, `/review me mid-year`
and `/review me year-end` use your supplied review form and sourced contributions.

Open [[Views/Reviews.base]] to browse saved formal reviews across people; it is not
a personal-only or permission-filtered view. `/status-update` and ordinary progress
reports remain available without a formal review. [[Setups/Work specializations]]
and [[Examples/Work walkthrough]] have optional ideas.
