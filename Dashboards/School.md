## Optional courses and terms

Open [[Views/School.base]] and select **Courses** for active courses across all terms,
or **Past courses** for completed/dropped courses. Filter term when needed; “active”
does not automatically mean the current term. Courses appear among active projects
above, but the detailed course tables are optional.

Enable school for `/course` and `/term-close`. A formal course uses the Course
project template, a supplied term, and source syllabus. Assignment is an optional
task template for coursework to submit. Preserve exact submission times/timezones
in Notes; due is the calendar date. Term closeout never completes unfinished work.

`/study` and Study session also work for personal learning or professional training
without a course or school setup. Use Note or `/report` for ordinary reflection.
Person is the default contact template. See [[Note types]], [[Skills]] and
[[Examples/School walkthrough]].
