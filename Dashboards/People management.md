# People management

This optional dashboard complements [[Dashboards/Work|the shared Work dashboard]].
Enable work + people-manager for coaching and reviews of confirmed reports.
The view itself never infers reports from titles, teams or assigned tasks.

## Your open tasks
![[Views/Work.base#My open]]

## Current direct reports
Only relationship report with current or blank status. Manager links are optional.
![[Views/People.base#My team]]

## Tasks assigned to current reports
Open work tasks whose linked assignees are current direct reports. Tasks assigned
to peers, vendors or former reports remain in the shared Work view; an assignment
does not prove that you delegated it or establish management authority.
![[Views/Work.base#Report tasks]]

## Optional coaching and evaluations

Use Direct report only for someone you manage; Person remains the default for
ordinary contacts and yourself. Employment/HR details are optional. `/1on1` is a
general conversation; `/coach` explicitly selects manager coaching. `/review me`
uses your own evidence; `/review Alex` requires a confirmed report and reviews on.
Manager self-evaluations distinguish your contribution from the team's result.
