# Personal

A focused view of personal tasks and projects: hobbies, learning, appointments,
errands, household responsibilities or anything else you choose. The personal area
does not require a home project, a purchase or a particular task type.

Dates/completions above concern non-recurring tasks; use TaskNotes for recurring
instances. Open [[Views/Personal.base]] for extra tabs, including **My open**. A task
with no area stays on [[Dashboard]] until you choose its context. Views read the
note's area directly; they do not infer it from a project or setup setting.

## Optional workflows

`/home` uses the personal setup for household planning. `/list`, `/item`, `/study`
and ordinary task/project commands work in any setup. Due is an actual deadline;
scheduled is your chosen day. Calendar events remain in your calendar.

[[Setups/Household|Household lists, loans, purchases and maintenance]] · [[Examples/Personal walkthrough|Walkthrough]]

Use Person for contacts and yourself; Professional contact and Direct report are
optional profiles for the corresponding context. See [[Note types]].
