# Work

A shared work dashboard for any role or domain: individual tasks, collaborative
projects, learning and follow-ups. It requires no tickets, employee records or
performance-review process. [[Dashboard|All areas]] · [[Setups/Dashboards|View guide]]

Dates/completions above concern non-recurring tasks; use TaskNotes for recurring
instances. Open [[Views/Work.base]] for extra tabs, including **My open**. A task
with no area stays on [[Dashboard]] until you choose its context. Views read the
note's area directly; they do not infer it from a project or setup setting.

Formal evaluations use [[Views/Reviews.base]] only when useful; `/review` and
`/evidence` require work + reviews, and reviews of others require a confirmed report
and people-manager role. An ordinary progress summary needs no review form.
