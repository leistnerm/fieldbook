description: Prep a 1:1 (/1on1 <person>)
---
Person: $ARGUMENTS (match to a person note). Today is !`powershell -NoProfile -Command "Get-Date -Format yyyy-MM-dd"`.

Gather:
1. Tasks with this person in `assignee`: open items with status, due, overdue
   flag, and last log line + date; open items with no log line in 14+ days;
   items done in the last 30 days.
2. Their last 3 meeting notes (by `date`, where `attendees` includes them):
   decisions, and any action items still open (unconverted lines, or linked
   tasks not done). Also any Commitments lines naming this person from the
   last 60 days in any meeting note.
3. From their person note: Goals & development, Feedback & recognition entries
   from the last 30 days, and any birthday or work anniversary in the next 30
   days.

End with a suggested agenda: blocked items, overdue, stale, open follow-ups
from past meetings, goals check-in, recognition to give. Don't edit any files.
Offer to create today's meeting note (where AGENTS.md says meetings go, named
`YYYY-MM-DD 1on1 <Person>`) from `Templates/Meeting.md` with the agenda filled
in. If a note with that name already exists, say so instead of creating one.
