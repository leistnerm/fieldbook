All areas share this dashboard, including notes with no area and notes from setups
you later disable. It shows your tasks and tasks assigned to others; check Assignee.
For only yours, open [[Views/Dashboard.base]] and select **My open**.

to act on instances. The **Recurring** tab in [[Views/Dashboard.base]] lists their
records; `/report` counts their recorded completions separately.
