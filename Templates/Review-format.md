<!-- General-purpose performance reflection. Adapt these prompts to the review
period and agreed responsibilities. Use linked evidence; leave unsupported claims
as questions. No organization-specific criteria or ratings are assumed.
Optional: add a Values: line listing the competencies you explicitly want covered.
-->

## Goals and responsibilities
<!-- What was agreed for this period? What changed, and why? Which goals remain
in progress? Describe scope and constraints without inventing expectations. -->

## Outcomes and impact
<!-- What was delivered or improved? Who benefited? Link examples and distinguish
your contribution from team results. Which measures support the claimed impact? -->

## Collaboration and approach
<!-- How did communication, judgment, reliability and collaboration affect the
result? What feedback supports this? Include concrete examples, not trait labels. -->

## Challenges and lessons
<!-- What did not go as planned? What was within your control? What did you learn,
and what would you do differently? Avoid attributing motives to other people. -->

## Learning and growth
<!-- Which skills or practices improved? Where were they applied? What further
practice, feedback, resources or support would help? -->

## Next-period priorities
<!-- Propose a small number of priorities with an intended outcome, a way to
measure progress and a review date. Mark proposals that need agreement. -->

## Discussion and support
<!-- What questions, tradeoffs or support requests should be discussed? Are there
responsibilities, dependencies or expectations that need clarification? -->

## Evidence gaps
<!-- Identify missing dates, sources, metrics or feedback. Ask before filling gaps.
Fictional examples are illustrations only and must never become review evidence. -->
