# Fieldbook 1.0.0

Initial public release.

- Shared Markdown records with optional personal, work and school setups.
- Guided onboarding, setup-aware templates and dashboards, daily and weekly reviews.
- Read-only tracker, calendar and Canvas LMS connections with local conflict previews.
- Configurable integration setup, credential guidance and connection tests.
- Verified vault backups, upgrade comparisons, preservation of customizations and restore.
- Plugin-free Python AI workflows with shared instructions and selectable harness profiles.
- Optional local MCP transport with separate local-write and remote-read opt-ins.
- Source package and configured starter vaults for Obsidian and optional AI assistant use.

- Versioned record templates and read-only template status inventory.
- Shared record-migration workflow: small previewed batches, verified backups and preservation of existing information and identities.
